Ordered-minor avoidance of stars, by center
==========================================

Statement
---------
Let S_{p,r} denote the star on the vertex set {1, 2, ..., p} with center r
(the other p - 1 vertices are leaves). Its Prufer code is the word r^{p-2}.
Write N(S_{p,r}, t) for the number of labeled trees on {1, 2, ..., t} that
avoid S_{p,r} as an ordered minor.

For integers r >= 1 and p > 2r,

    N(S_{p,r}, t) > N(S_{p,r+1}, t)

for every t >= p + 1. Label reversal gives the identity

    N(S_{p,r}, t) = N(S_{p, p+1-r}, t),

so the inequality is the statement that the counts fall strictly as the
center moves from an end toward the middle of {1, 2, ..., p}.

The cases t = p + 1 and t = p + 2 are proved. This file records every exact
computation available for t >= p + 3. Every such comparison holds.

How the counts were obtained
----------------------------
Two enumerations are used, and they agree wherever both apply.

Census. An exhaustive contraction search counts N(P, t) for every labeling P
of every tree on p vertices, for the host orders listed under Sources. A line
marked Symmetry or Complement in a log was copied by label reversal and is
not an independent enumeration of that center. Every comparison in the main
table is between two directly enumerated values.

Leaf scan. Contraction cannot create a leaf, so a tree with at most p - 2
leaves avoids every star on p vertices, and a tree with at least p leaves
contains every such star. The only trees that can avoid one p-star and
contain another have exactly p - 1 leaves. star_order_scan enumerates those
trees and counts the avoiders B_r(t) of each center. The closed form

    N(S_{p,r}, t) = B_r(t) + sum_{ell = 2}^{p - 2} (t! / ell!) * S(t - 2, t - ell)

recovers the full count. S(n, k) is the Stirling number of the second kind.
The sum is the number of labeled trees on [t] with at most p - 2 leaves;
it does not depend on r. For p = 3 the sum is empty.

The leaf scan was checked against the census on every center of every
overlapping pair (t, p) with 4 <= p <= 8. All 102 counts match.
At p = 8 and t = 10, which is the last proved order t = p + 2, the scan
matches the completed run results_t10_p8.txt at all eight centers. The
interrupted log results_t10_contraction_fixed.txt contains a killed-process
fragment at the center-2 star and is not used for that order.

For p = 8 and t = 11 the census reached centers 1 and 2 only
(results_t11_p8.txt). The leaf scan supplies the remaining centers. Its
counts at centers 1 and 2 equal the census. A later incomplete census row is
filled from the leaf scan only when every center the census reached agrees
with the scan.
